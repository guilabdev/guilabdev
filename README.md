## 🟡 About Me

I'm **Gui Barboza**, a developer in training focused on building a strong foundation in programming and moving toward **Backend Development**.

I like looking at real-world problems, thinking about possible solutions, building prototypes, testing them, and improving what works.

I'm not interested in being limited to one programming language. I want to explore different technologies, challenge myself, and understand how systems work behind the interface.

> **Think. Build. Test. Understand. Improve.**
